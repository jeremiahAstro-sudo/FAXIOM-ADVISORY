Meet the lead consultant

Emmanuel Olaitan, ACA

Managing Partner, Faxiom Advisory Limited

Emmanuel Olaitan is a finance and business advisory professional with a background in Accounting and Finance, chartered accountancy, inventory management, costing, financial reporting, tax matters and operational controls. He holds a B.Sc. in Accounting & Finance, is an Associate Chartered Accountant (ACA) of ICAN, an AAT and a Certified Sage Advisor.

His experience spans inventory control and costing, weighted-average costing, multi-location stock verification, finance process improvement, accounting systems implementation and practical Excel-based finance training. He has facilitated learning for approximately 800 finance professionals and has managed teams of more than 30 accounting and finance professionals across client engagements.

As Managing Partner of Faxiom Advisory Limited, Emmanuel focuses on helping business owners and management teams strengthen financial visibility, improve compliance, investigate operational gaps and translate financial information into clear decisions. His approach combines professional rigour with commercial awareness and plain-language communication.

  • Accounting, finance and management reporting
  • Tax advisory, compliance support and tax issue reviews
  • Inventory management, stock verification and costing
  • Internal audit, reconciliation and operational controls
  • Finance team training and accounting process improvement

Which businesses do you work with?

Faxiom can discuss support for SMEs, growing companies, professional services, trading and distribution businesses, property businesses and other organisations. The best fit depends on the scope and requirements.

Yes. Depending on the agreed scope, we can provide an independent review, specialist tax or inventory support, training, reconciliation assistance or management-reporting improvement.

How much do your services cost?

Fees depend on the work required, transaction volume, locations, complexity, timeline and deliverables. We recommend a scoped proposal before work begins.

Can you help with an urgent tax or audit query?

Share the relevant deadline, authority or issue and available records. We will assess whether the request fits our capacity and agree next steps. Outcomes depend on the facts, applicable law and evidence available.
